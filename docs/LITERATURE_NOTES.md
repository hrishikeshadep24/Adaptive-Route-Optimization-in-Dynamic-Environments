- Huang & Lu 2020: online dynamic courier routing; heuristic insertion, adaptive memory, simulated annealing; compared with greedy; reports response time; best strategy depends on dynamism level; future work mentions hyper-heuristic insertion. => "dynamic insertion + SA" is NOT novel.
- Costa et al. 2024: dynamic VRP, new demands after initial routes; DSNR with 2-Opt* beats QRP Sweep and K-Means Greedy on Loggibud. => dynamic 2-Opt* style repair is NOT novel.
- Liu & Wang 2026: traffic-aware ALNS + layered Tabu memory + rollout dispatch; reports response time, quality, robustness, ablation. Too large to reproduce in this project.
- Kolachalam & Malhotra 2026: greedy heuristics vs ALNS, GA, SA under identical conditions; emphasizes reward-vs-compute frontier. => measure computation, not only final quality.
- A curated 60-paper 2019-2026 routing corpus exists (spreadsheet kept outside the repo); recurring evaluation gaps: inconsistent dynamism scales, inconsistent compute budgets, limited cross-instance-family robustness, incomplete response-time reporting, non-standardized multi-objective evaluation.
- Targeted gaps (methodology gaps, not claims dynamic routing is unexplored): (1) fair computational comparison incl. update/selection overhead, (2) controlled reproducible dynamicity curve, (3) cross-instance robustness with held-out families, (4) quality vs response (cost, update time, effort, feasibility, disruption), (5) evidence-based adaptation (build the selector only if data justify).
